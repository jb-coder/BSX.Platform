# ADR-003 - Domain Driven Design

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

BSX Platform is centered around business domains.

Business logic must remain protected from technical concerns.

---

## Decision

Adopt Tactical Domain Driven Design.

Use:

- Entities
- Aggregates
- Value Objects
- Domain Events

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Better domain modeling
- Clear business language
- Rich business models

### Negative

- Initial learning curve

---

## Notes

DDD must remain pragmatic.
