# BSX Platform Development Principles

## Principle 1

Prefer business clarity over technical cleverness.

---

## Principle 2

Optimize for maintainability over development speed.

---

## Principle 3

Duplicated code is acceptable.

Duplicated business rules are not.

---

## Principle 4

Avoid abstractions until multiple implementations exist.

---

## Principle 5

Every module should remain understandable in isolation.

---

## Principle 6

Business rules belong to the Domain.

---

## Principle 7

Architecture is a product feature.

Protect it at all costs.

---

## Principle 8

Code generated by AI must be reviewed.

Never assume generated code is correct.

---

## Principle 9

Every architectural decision must have a documented reason.

---

## Principle 10

Future maintainability is more important than short-term productivity.
