# BSX Platform Business Glossary

## Customer

An organization or individual purchasing products or services.

---

## Contact

A person associated with a customer.

---

## Product

A sellable item managed by Inventory.

---

## Warehouse

A physical or virtual location where stock is stored.

---

## Stock Movement

A business event that changes inventory levels.

---

## Quotation

A commercial offer made to a customer.

---

## Sales Order

A confirmed customer purchase request.

---

## Invoice

A financial document generated from a sales transaction.

---

## Supplier

An organization providing goods or services.

---

## Purchase Order

A purchasing request sent to a supplier.

---

## User

A person allowed to access BSX Platform.

---

## Role

A collection of permissions.

---

## Permission

A granular action that can be granted to a user.
