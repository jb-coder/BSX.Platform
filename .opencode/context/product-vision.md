# BSX Platform Vision

## Mission

Build a modern enterprise business platform capable of unifying business operations through a modular and scalable architecture.

BSX Platform is not just an ERP.

The long-term objective is to create a Business Operating System that can support multiple business domains through installable and loosely coupled modules.

Examples:

- CRM
- Sales
- Inventory
- Purchasing
- Accounting
- Human Resources
- Projects
- Manufacturing
- Analytics
- AI

---

## Core Principles

### Simplicity

Prefer simple solutions over complex abstractions.

### Scalability

Design for future growth without introducing premature complexity.

### Maintainability

Future developers should quickly understand the codebase.

### Modularity

Every module must evolve independently.

### Extensibility

The platform must support adding new modules with minimal impact.

### Product Quality

Every feature must be developed with production quality standards.

---

## Long-Term Goals

- Multi-company support
- SaaS readiness
- AI integration
- Mobile support
- Public API
- Third-party integrations
- Marketplace-ready architecture

---

## Product Philosophy

BSX Platform should feel closer to:

- Linear
- Stripe
- Notion
- Vercel

than traditional ERP systems.

The objective is creating modern business software.
